=== I002 reproduction (subpath .. and ; segments accepted) ===
Input: pkg:npm/x#..
Parsed subpath: '..'

Additional cases:
  'pkg:npm/foo#../../../etc/passwd'                  -> subpath='../../../etc/passwd'
  'pkg:npm/foo#a;b;../../etc/passwd'                 -> subpath='a;b;../../etc/passwd'
  'pkg:npm/foo#'                                     -> subpath=''
  'pkg:npm/foo#normal/path'                          -> subpath='normal/path'

Parser is data-only: subpath may contain .., ;, and arbitrary segments
A consumer that interprets subpath for filesystem I/O must sanitize itself
